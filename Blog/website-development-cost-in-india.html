Website Development Cost in India: How Much Should You Pay in 2026?

Realistic price ranges for business, e-commerce and custom websites — plus the hidden costs most quotes don't mention.

What Does Website Development Cost in India in 2026?

"How much for a website?" is like asking "how much for a vehicle" — a cycle and a truck both have wheels, but they are not the same purchase. Website quotes in India range from ₹5,000 page-builder setups to ₹15 lakh+ custom platforms, and every number in between can be legitimate depending on what is actually being built.

To give you real anchors instead of vague hand-waving — consistent with the starting prices we publish openly — here is what each class of website generally costs in the Indian market:

Website typeTypical cost rangeWhat you get
Basic business website (template/CMS)₹15,000 – ₹50,0005–10 pages, contact forms, mobile-responsive, basic SEO setup
Professional custom website₹50,000 – ₹2,00,000Custom design, CMS, faster performance, stronger SEO foundations
E-commerce website₹1,00,000 – ₹5,00,000Product catalog, cart, payment gateway, order management
Custom web application / portal₹3,00,000 – ₹15,00,000+Business logic, logins, databases, integrations, dashboards

For international clients, our published starting point for business websites is $1,000 — the same work scoped against global rates rather than Indian ones.

Key takeawayThe price is driven less by "how many pages" and more by how much of the site is custom, who writes the content, what it must integrate with, and what happens after launch. Judge every quote against those four variables.

7 Factors That Change What You'll Pay

  1. Template vs custom design. A themed WordPress site costs a fraction of a bespoke design system. Custom design is worth it when brand differentiation drives revenue; templates are fine when speed and budget dominate.
  2. Who creates the content. If the company writes your copy, takes photos and sources images, that work is priced in. Supplying your own content can cut costs meaningfully.
  3. Number and complexity of features. Booking systems, payment gateways, customer logins, multi-language support and calculators each add build and testing time.
  4. Integrations. Connecting to CRMs, ERPs, shipping APIs, WhatsApp Business or accounting tools requires development beyond the visible website.
  5. E-commerce depth. A 20-product store is simple; a 5,000-SKU catalog with filters, variant pricing and bulk upload is a different project.
  6. CMS and admin needs. Editing text yourself is standard; complex role-based workflows for teams add cost.
  7. Who builds it. Freelancers are cheapest, small agencies sit mid-range, and established companies with dedicated designers, developers and QA cost more — and carry more accountability.

Hidden Costs Most Quotes Don't Mention

  • Domain and hosting: ₹1,000–₹8,000/year for domains, ₹3,000–₹30,000+/year for hosting depending on traffic and infrastructure.
  • Premium plugins/themes: ₹3,000–₹20,000/year in licenses for CMS-based sites.
  • SSL certificate: Often free (Let's Encrypt), but some setups charge.
  • Payment gateway: Setup is usually free in India; transaction fees run ~2% per sale.
  • Maintenance: Updates, backups, security patches — typically ₹2,000–₹15,000/month or an annual contract.
  • The next phase: Websites that generate leads need content and SEO work after launch. Budget for it, or the site will quietly decay.

Ask any vendor to state which of these are included. A quote that is silent on hosting and maintenance is not cheaper — it is incomplete.

How to Compare Website Quotes (Without Getting Fooled)

  1. Demand an itemized scope: pages, features, integrations, who supplies content, revision rounds.
  2. Ask what happens after launch: bug-fix window, support terms, and whether you own the code and accounts.
  3. Check the portfolio for depth, not logos: open their past sites on your phone. Slow or broken work speaks louder than a client list.
  4. Verify the SEO foundation: the site should ship with proper titles, meta tags, schema and fast load times — retrofitting SEO later costs more. See our guide to getting your website on Google.
  5. Benchmark against transparent pricing: vendors confident in their value publish starting numbers — compare with our pricing page.

DIY vs Hiring a Website Development Company

DIY page builders work for validating an idea or a pure-placeholder presence. They fail businesses the moment leads matter: slow templates, weak SEO control, no custom functionality, and hours of your own time.

A professional company earns its cost when your website is a revenue channel — lead generation, online sales, or client credibility. Common questions

Website Cost FAQs

Quick answers to what Indian businesses ask about website pricing.

How much does a website cost in India in 2026?

A basic business website typically costs ₹15,000–₹50,000, a professional custom website ₹50,000–₹2,00,000, an e-commerce website ₹1,00,000–₹5,00,000, and a custom web application ₹3,00,000+ depending on features and complexity.

Why do website quotes vary so much between vendors?

Quotes differ because of design approach (template vs custom), who builds it (freelancer vs agency), content responsibility, integrations, and whether long-term support is included. Always compare itemized deliverables, not headline prices.

What hidden costs come with a new website?

Domain renewal, hosting, SSL, premium plugins or themes, stock images, payment gateway fees, ongoing maintenance, and future feature additions are recurring or follow-on costs to plan for.

Is a ₹5,000 website worth it?

A ₹5,000 website is usually a DIY page builder setup with a generic template, no custom design, weak SEO foundations and no support. It can work as a temporary placeholder, but businesses that rely on their website for leads typically outgrow it quickly.
