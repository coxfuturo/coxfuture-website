Mobile App Development Cost in India: A Realistic 2026 Guide

What apps actually cost at each complexity level, which decisions move the price most, and the ongoing costs most budgets forget.

CoxFuture Editorial Team22 September 202610 min read

On this page

What Does Mobile App Development Cost in India in 2026?

App pricing in India is among the most competitive in the world, which is good news — and also why quotes for the same idea can range from ₹1 lakh to ₹40 lakh. The difference is almost never the screen count. It is the amount of custom engineering behind the screens: the backend, integrations, security and the quality process.

Here are realistic market ranges, consistent with the starting prices we publish:

App complexityTypical cost rangeExamples
Simple app₹2,00,000 – ₹5,00,000Content apps, basic business apps, static catalogs, simple booking
Mid-complexity app₹5,00,000 – ₹12,00,000User accounts, payments, real-time chat, admin dashboards, APIs
Complex app / platform₹12,00,000 – ₹40,00,000+Marketplaces, on-demand platforms, fintech, heavy custom backend

For international clients, our published starting points are $20,000 for native Android or iOS and $35,000 for cross-platform builds — the same engineering scoped to global rates.

Key takeawayAn app is a product, not a purchase. The build is usually 60–70% of first-year cost; servers, maintenance and marketing make up the rest. Budget for the full lifecycle, not just the launch.

What Drives App Development Costs

  1. Custom backend and business logic. The single biggest cost driver. A content app with no server is cheap; anything with accounts, payments, or real-time features needs real backend engineering.
  2. Integrations. Payment gateways, maps, SMS/WhatsApp, ERP/CRM connections, video streaming — each adds development and testing time.
  3. Design depth. Stock components versus a designed brand experience differ by lakhs. Custom UI matters most in consumer-facing products.
  4. Security and compliance. Fintech, health and apps handling payments need hardened security, encryption and often compliance work — non-negotiable, and priced accordingly.
  5. Offline mode, sync and scale. Apps that must work offline, sync across devices, or serve lakhs of concurrent users need architecture planning upfront.
  6. Team composition. Freelancers build simple apps well; multi-role teams (designer, Android, iOS, backend, QA, PM) are required as scope grows.

Android, iOS or Cross-Platform: The Cost Impact

Building natively for both platforms means two frontend codebases — roughly double the frontend effort. Cross-platform frameworks (Flutter, React Native) share one codebase across both, typically landing at 1.2–1.5x the cost of a single platform instead of 2x.

Practical guidance: Android-only makes sense when your audience research shows Android dominance (common in India); iOS-first when targeting premium urban segments abroad. For most businesses needing both, cross-platform delivers the best cost-to-reach ratio. Our detailed comparison of native vs cross-platform development breaks down when each approach wins, and our teams build all three — see Android, iOS and cross-platform development.

Ongoing Costs After Launch

  • Servers and infrastructure: ₹5,000–₹1,00,000+/month depending on users and data (₹20,000/month is typical for a growing mid-size app).
  • Maintenance: OS updates, library upgrades, bug fixes — plan 15–20% of the build cost per year.
  • Store fees: Google Play $25 one-time; Apple App Store $99/year.
  • Third-party services: SMS, push notifications, maps, payment processing — per-use charges.
  • Growth: ASO, ads, content. An unmarketed app is a diary, not a product.

How to Budget Smartly (And Avoid the Two Classic Mistakes)

Mistake 1: Building everything in v1. Every feature you defer makes v1 cheaper and faster to validate. Launch with the core loop — the one thing users must do — and add features based on real usage. An MVP at ₹3–5 lakh that validates demand beats a ₹15 lakh launch that guesses.

Mistake 2: Choosing the cheapest quote. Apps have long lifecycles; badly structured code becomes more expensive every month you keep it. Evaluate vendors on process (design docs, sprint demos, test coverage, code handover), not just price. Our guide on evaluating a development company covers the questions that expose the difference.

Common questions

App Cost FAQs

Quick answers to what businesses ask about app development pricing in India.

How much does it cost to develop a mobile app in India?

A simple app typically costs ₹2,00,000–₹5,00,000, a mid-complexity app ₹5,00,000–₹12,00,000, and a complex app with custom backend, integrations or marketplace features ₹12,00,000–₹40,00,000+.

Does it cost more to build for both Android and iOS?

Building separately for both platforms roughly doubles frontend work. Cross-platform frameworks like Flutter or React Native reduce that to about 1.2–1.5x of a single platform while sharing most of the codebase.

What are the ongoing costs after an app launches?

Server and infrastructure costs, maintenance and OS updates, the Google Play $25 one-time fee and Apple's $99/year developer fee, plus marketing. Budget 15–20% of the build cost annually for maintenance.

How long does it take to develop an app?

A simple app takes 2–3 months, a mid-complexity app 3–5 months, and complex platforms 6–9 months, depending on scope, integrations and testing depth.
